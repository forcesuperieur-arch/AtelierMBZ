One-line: the refusal that says your ceiling, names who decides above it, and leaves both ways on.

```jsx
<PermissionCallout
  title="Cette remise dépasse votre plafond"
  description="Vous pouvez accorder jusqu'à 15 %. Au-delà, la validation revient à la direction."
  requestLabel="Demander à Pascal M."
  complyLabel="Ramener à 15 %"
  note="La demande part par notification, avec le motif que vous avez saisi."
/>
```

- `description` carries the number and the person or the body that decides beyond it. « Accès refusé » leaves nobody to ask and nothing to do.
- Keep both paths: ask, and come back inside the limit. Either one alone turns a decision into a dead end.
- `note` says what asking sets in motion, so the user knows whether something has just left their hands.
