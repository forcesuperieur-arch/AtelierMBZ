One-line: the uppercase severity word at the end of a queue row or bay tile, and the round counter next to a section title.

```jsx
<StatusBadge tone="error">Critique</StatusBadge>
<StatusBadge tone="warning">À surveiller</StatusBadge>
<Counter tone="accent">7</Counter>
```

- The badge carries the word; the row's left edge carries the colour. Both, always.
